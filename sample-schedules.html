Sample Schedules

Wondering what an actual session looks like? Here are the sample timelines we'll use for the summer pilot days, a private D&D one-shot, a birthday party, and a family game night. Every booking flexes a bit based on age range, group size, and what your family wants — email us and we'll build a plan around it.

Summer Pilot Program · Aug 21 & 28, 2026

A Pilot Day

Full day at Lanesville Community Center, ages 6–13, running alongside our Junior Facilitators.

  • 9:00amWelcome, check-in, name tags, free play
  • 10:00amBreak into small groups by age — first game / adventure starts
  • 11:00amReflection on first game
  • 12:00pmLunch, free social time
  • 12:45pmSecond game / adventure continues
  • 2:30pmReflection circle / large group game
  • 3:00pmFarewell and pickup

Please plan to pick up promptly at 3:00pm. If you'll be late, just let us know in advance.

Private D&D One-Shot · 2.5–3 hours

A Private D&D Session

Up to a group of 6 children or adults.

Booked at your preferred location, with pre-generated characters — no prior D&D experience needed. Ages 8+ — some light reading and math skills (addition/subtraction) are helpful, and we can help!

  • 0:00Arrival, character selection
  • 0:10Intro: what's a one-shot, how the dice work
  • 0:20DM-guided adventure begins
  • 2:00Break
  • 2:10Adventure wraps up, quick reflection on favorite moments and lessons learned
Birthday Party · 2–2.5 hours

A Birthday Party

Group size determines game selection options — 4–30 children or adults.

Gaming plus the party essentials — the birthday kid picks the games and goes first in the welcome circle.

  • 0:00Arrival, welcome circle, quick how-to-play for guests who don't all know each other
  • 0:10Game 1 — a collaborative icebreaker game
  • 0:55Cake break
  • 1:15Game 2 — birthday kid's choice
  • 1:50Closing circle — shout-outs for the birthday kid's guests
Family Game Night · ~2 hours

A Family Game Night

Multi-generational play — parents and grandparents at the table as players, not just spectators.

  • 0:00Game intro and setup
  • 0:15Game 1 — easy for mixed ages and lower stakes
  • 1:00Break
  • 1:15Game 2 — a slightly more strategic option for families who want a challenge
  • 1:55Closing — a quick round of favorite moments
