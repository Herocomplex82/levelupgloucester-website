Mission Driven Play

Level Up Gloucester is a mission-driven community program that positions gaming not just as entertainment, but as a primary vehicle for teaching essential social and cognitive skills to the next generation.

We believe in the power of facilitated play. Through structured tabletop gaming and community events, we provide an accessible, educational environment where children develop soft skills, leadership, communication, and conflict resolution in real-time.

Starting October 2026, we're partnering with the Cape Ann YMCA to bring this programming into their after-school program.

Meet Our Founder

Higher education leadership educator, dad of two, and Dungeon Master of 25 years.

Steve Rossi has spent his career in higher education as a leadership and social justice educator, building programs and large-scale events that help young people find their voice and their community. He brings that same experience to Level Up Gloucester — decades of watching students grow into confident, engaged leaders, now aimed at kids just starting that journey.

A father of two and a Dungeon Master for 25 years, Steve has run more campaigns around the kitchen table than he can count, and he's seen firsthand what a well-run game can teach: how to listen, how to problem-solve under pressure, and how to root for the person next to you. Level Up Gloucester grew out of that belief — that play, done with intention, is one of the most powerful tools we have for building leaders and community.

Our Programs

Summer Pilot Program

Two trial Fridays — August 21 & 28, 9am–3pm at Lanesville Community Center. Board games, card games, and guided Dungeons & Dragons adventures for kids ages 6-13. $65/child, sliding scale available — no child turned away.

After-school Programming

Ongoing youth gaming sessions held after school hours that focus on developing life skills through structured tabletop play.

School Partnerships

Collaborative programs that bring tabletop gaming curriculum and life-skills development directly to local schools and organizations.

Private D&D & Family Game Nights

Facilitated, private tabletop game sessions tailored for families, birthday groups, or small circles of friends at their preferred location.
