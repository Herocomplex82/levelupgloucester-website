Board of Directors

Level Up Gloucester is guided by a founding board that brings together community programming experience, clinical expertise, and municipal know-how.

Steve Rossi

Founder & Executive Director

A Gloucester resident and Plum Cove Elementary PTO member with a career in higher education as a leadership and social justice educator, alongside community programming and tabletop game instruction experience, including specialty board game retail with Game Knight. Holds training in social-emotional learning facilitation.

Shruti Tekwani

Board Member

A Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC) in private practice and a school counselor at the International School of Curaçao. Shruti brings clinical grounding to our social-emotional learning framework and volunteer training.

Frank Margiotta

Board Member · Finance & Governance

Ward 4 City Councilor for the City of Gloucester, with a B.S. in Business from Johnson & Wales University. Frank brings financial oversight and deep municipal relationships to the organization.
